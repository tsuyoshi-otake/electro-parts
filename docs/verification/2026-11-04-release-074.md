# 0.7.4 release: the store prices always under a full-width chart (#71, PR #72 and PR #73)

On a Switch Science product page the chart was squeezed to about half the panel, with the store prices in a narrow right column (#71). The panel had put the two side by side as soon as its inner width passed 700px. 0.7.4 went through three designs on 2026-11-04:
1. PR #72 (8be0b82, merged, never on Pages): side by side only from an inner width of 960px.
2. On the owner's request, 800px. Not merged.
3. The owner then dropped side by side altogether (「右に出すレイアウトは全面ハイシで」「下でいいか」). PR #73 (11b778a) ships this design:
   - the chart always spans the panel;
   - the viewed store's card leads the same wrapping grid as the other stores' cards (each card at least 200px wide);
   - a product without other stores puts its statistics beside the current price from an inner width of 560px.

This record is the pre-submission check that CLAUDE.md requires, run on the ZIP that Pages serves. The evidence is kept under `~/tmp/electro-074/`:
- `pages-before/` and `pages-after/`: the store manifests and state, and the published ZIP;
- `main-ext.zip`: built from the merge commit for the byte comparison;
- `pub-ext/`: the published ZIP's extraction;
- `live-layout.mjs` with `live-below/` (the local ZIP) and `live-pub/` (the published ZIP): the layout check;
- `live.mjs` with `live-func/`: the 0.7.3 functional script, whose selectors now read the `.current-price` card.

Every checked page met all of these:
- the chart was above the prices and as wide as the panel body;
- there was no horizontal overflow;
- no 取得できませんでした or 読み込み中 text was left;
- a chart `svg` was present after the panel was scrolled into view.

The page errors (19 in the local run, 17 in the published run) all came from the stores' own scripts: Switch Science `empire.js` `_updatePrice` and M5Stack Goaffpro. None came from the extension.
