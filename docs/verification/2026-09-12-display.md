# 0.4.6 local Playwright display verification

Issue: #25. Verified on Windows on 2026-09-12.

## Finding and fix

The published 0.4.5 single-store chart used a fixed 760px SVG viewBox. At a 390px viewport its 11px axis labels rendered at 4.69px. The single-store chart now measures its container and redraws on resize, with observer cleanup and no additional data requests. At the same viewport, 0.4.6 renders the labels at 11px (324 × 220 chart). The comparison chart remains readable at 356 × 220.
