## What this release does not change

- The data contract (v1.1), the publisher, the collectors, the config and the workflows. The estimate and the window are computed in the panel from the stock-count and availability series that were already published; clients before 0.7.8 simply lack the buttons and the estimate.
- The default view: the whole record, as before. The chosen period is not kept across pages.

## What the estimate is

The sum of the decreases between consecutive recorded stock counts inside the window. It is a guide, neither an upper nor a lower bound: sales and restocking between two observations cancel, and a returned or corrected count is read as a sale when it lowers the count. A missing count is read as zero only while the offer is out of stock; a count the store shows capped (`100+`) turns the figure into 「N個以上」.

The first `npm run cws:dashboard -- fill` printed `saved` for both tabs, but the immediate `check` read 単一用途の説明 (299 chars) and storage の理由 (142 chars) as the 0.7.7 wording, which lacks 「推定販売数」. On the privacy tab `fill` puts three fields in one after another and then presses 「下書きとして保存する」; the dashboard autosaves on blur (「アイテムを保存しています…」) and the screenshot taken while saving showed the header save button disabled. Filling one field, waiting about 3 s, pressing save and reloading kept the value (305 chars, then 148 chars), and `check` then read all four fields `same`. The cause was inferred from that behaviour, not traced in the dashboard. `fill` still has no wait for the save to finish and no read-back per field; `check` after `fill` is what caught it, and the checklist keeps `check` as a required step before `blockers`.
