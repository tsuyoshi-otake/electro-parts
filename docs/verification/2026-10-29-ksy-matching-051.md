Scope, counts, the four unresolved pairs and what is out of scope are in [three-store-mapping.md](../roadmap/three-store-mapping.md#051-ksyの追加2026-10-29判定); the pipeline decisions are in [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-reviewed-catalog-mapping-pipeline.md#051-raspberry-pi-shop-by-ksyの追加).

## Reviews

- 324 KSY candidates in five maker batches, one Claude Sonnet 5.5 subagent each. A merge script checked every row: id and fingerprint equal to the candidate, decision in the enum, evidence of at least 40 characters, the price-condition note on every same product, missing evidence on every unresolved pair, and the reviewed offer on every official M5Stack variant. 0 problems.
- Integrating review (Claude Opus 5.5): all 324 rows read as a table (decision, code, both names, differences); nine random rows and every questioned row (Pi 5 SC1110, RP2350A/B reels, the two Grove connector packs, M5Stack K001/U040/U078-D) compared with the catalogue fields. One decision changed: SS 10908 / KSY 1095 from rejected to unresolved, the same as the Akizuki precedent `a129606-s10908`; the original decision is kept in its evidence.

Found by the suites and fixed:

- `typecheck` failed with TS2590 ("union type that is too complex") on `PRODUCT_RELATIONS`: the generated array of 1,356 object-literal spreads made TypeScript form one union of every row's type (1,032 rows still passed). `serializeRelations` now emits each row through a typed `r(relation, a, b)`, so each row is checked on its own.
- `relations.test.ts` checked every endpoint by a canonical link, but the KSY adapter reads the product only from the open dialog. KSY endpoints are now checked on the saved KSY page with the dialog opened at the relation URL (shared `openKsyProduct` helper).
- E2E: the Akizuki Pico page has a second identity card (KSY 634), so the store-price assertion selects the SS relation by id and checks the KSY card. The KSY extension spec answers 404 for products absent from the saved site, as Pages does, and checks the Akizuki and SS Pico 2 cards and the bounded related requests.
