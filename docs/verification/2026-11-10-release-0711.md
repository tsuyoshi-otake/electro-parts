# 0.7.10 and 0.7.11 release: Pimoroni in the cross-store mapping, history loads for the variant on screen, Akizuki bare maker numbers (#94, #114, #113)

0.7.10 (PR #115) added Pimoroni to the reviewed cross-store mapping and made a product with several variants load the other shops' histories for the variant on screen (#114). 0.7.11 (PR #116) tied the Akizuki rows that carry a bare Adafruit, Pololu or Kitronik number to the other shops through reviewed aliases (#113). 0.7.10 was merged but never published on its own: this one publish and this one check cover both versions. The decisions are in the 0.7.10 and 0.7.11 sections of [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-reviewed-catalog-mapping-pipeline.md), the counts in [店舗間の照合範囲](../roadmap/three-store-mapping.md).

This record is the pre-submission check that CLAUDE.md requires. The evidence is kept under `~/tmp/electro-0711/`: `live.mjs` (the 0.7.9 script plus C27, C28 and C29; C25 now asks only that no related section disappeared, since both versions add relations), `zip/pub.zip` and `pub-ext/` (the ZIP Pages serves), `live-pub/` (screenshots and `results.json`), `live-run.txt`.

## The new checks

- **C27 (#94), a Pimoroni row on another shop's page**: Akizuki 117209 (ATOM Lite) lists Switch Science, Sengoku, M5Stack and Pimoroni. The Pimoroni row reads 「約 ￥1,751」 with 「記録価格 £8.25」, 「送料・関税別のため円の店舗の後に表示」 and 「参考記録価格 · 販売単位・付属品等の比較条件が未確認」, and has no cheapest badge and no price difference.
- **C28 (#114), the variant on screen**: Pimoroni's Raspberry Pi 5 opened on 8GB (the shop's default). Its four rows (Akizuki, Switch Science, Sengoku, KSY) all showed a recorded price, none stayed reference-only. After choosing 4GB, the four rows of 4GB all showed a recorded price too, with 4 product requests after the switch. The screenshot of 4GB shows Akizuki 「Raspberry Pi 5 4GB」 ￥22,330, KSY 「SC1111 Raspberry Pi 5/4GB」 ￥21,780, Switch Science 「Raspberry Pi 5 / 4GB」 ￥22,990, and Sengoku folded under 「他の店舗（1件）」.
- **C29 (#113), an Akizuki page tied through an alias**: Akizuki 116356 (model field 「3954」) shows the Pimoroni card 「Adafruit NeoTrellis RGB Driver PCB for 4x4 Keypad · ADA3954」, 「約 ￥2,175」, 「記録価格 £10.25」, linking to the Pimoroni variant URL, without a difference or a badge. Before 0.7.11 this page had no other-shop card.
- **C25, no related section lost since the 0.7.9 run**: the same headings on six pages; two grew with the new relations (Akizuki 117209 「類似商品」 6 → 7件, Switch Science 11349 2件・1種類 → 4件・2種類); three pages without a section in both runs.
- The earlier checks still pass: initial light theme, saved dark theme after reload, series toggle, period buttons and estimate (hand count of sold and added units), pointer readout on every chart, yen headline and card fee, 63 data responses all 2xx/304, one exchange-rate request for the whole run, no `related products unavailable` warning.
