# 0.7.2 release: Pages ZIP on live store pages (#65, #67, PR #66, PR #68)

This is the pre-submission check of the 0.7.2 extension ZIP that Pages serves, as CLAUDE.md requires before a Chrome Web Store submission. 0.7.2 contains:
- 0.7.1 (#65), which was merged but never published on its own: the panel's typography, a 12 px text floor, hover highlights only where the pointer hovers, and card options that lead with the rate;
- the one-column placement (#67).

The evidence is kept under `~/tmp/electro-072/`:
- Scripts:
  - `live-placement.mjs`: placement at each width;
  - `pages-check.mjs`: Pages before and after the run;
  - reused from earlier releases: `~/tmp/electro-071/desktop-audit.mjs` (typography and layout) and `~/tmp/electro-070/live.mjs` (functional checks).
- The published ZIP and its extraction: `pages-after/ext.zip` and `pub-ext/`.
- `local-ext.zip`, built from the merge commit's tree for the byte comparison.
- The store manifests and state before and after the run: `pages-before/` and `pages-after/`.
- The run outputs, each with screenshots and its JSON:
  - `live-pub/` (placement);
  - `audit-pub/` (typography and layout);
  - `live-func/` (functional).

## Live pages

The ZIP was extracted fresh and loaded in Playwright with `channel: 'chromium'` and an empty profile for each script. Nothing was routed or replaced. The checks opened one product page per store at each width, plus the functional pages of the 0.7.0 record. Nothing else was crawled.

### Placement (`live-placement.mjs`, 1920 / 1440 / 1280 / 1024 / 768 / 390)

"Gap" is how far the panel's top is below the purchase button, in px. At 390 px the script uses a phone user agent.

| Store | Two columns | One column |
|---|---|---|
| Pimoroni Presto | 1920–1280: after the purchase column in the left column, gap 444–900 px. With the panel's top at 150 px, the sticky Add to cart is on screen beside it | 1024 / 768 / 390: right after `#add_to_cart`, gap 50 / 50 / 69 |
| M5Stack ATOM Lite | 1920–1024: beside the purchase column, as in 0.7.1 | 768 / 390: the product form's last child, gap 113 / 126 |
| Akizuki 117209 | 1920–768 (desktop page): 166 px under the purchase box, as in 0.7.1 | 390 (smartphone page): right after `#SalesArea`, gap 147 |
| Switch Science 10978 | 107 px under the button at every width (unchanged) | — |
| Sengoku EEHD-5LAU | 149–161 px under the cart button at every width (unchanged) | — |

- Crossing each breakpoint both ways moved the same host:
  - Pimoroni 1100 ↔ 1000: between `aside.purchase` and `div.about`, then after `#add_to_cart`;
  - M5Stack 800 ↔ 700: before `.product-description`, then inside the form.
- No page had horizontal overflow, except Akizuki's desktop page at 1024 (56 px) and 768 (312 px). That is the store's own 1,080 px layout and is the same without the extension.
- Only the stores' own fixed headers and floating buttons cover the panel while scrolling.

### Typography and layout (`desktop-audit.mjs`, 1920 / 1440 / 1280 / 1024 / 390, light and dark, 50 captures)

On all five stores:
- the panel text is system-ui 13 px / 20.8 px, and no text is under 12 px;
- no y-axis label is clipped and the panel does not overflow;
- in dark mode, page and panel are both `rgb(62, 62, 62)`;
- the settings select shows the whole 「3.63%　三井住友カード（Visa・Mastercard）」 at 1440 and 390.

At 390 the header is on one row except on M5Stack and Pimoroni, where the panel is now inside the purchase box and narrower. There 設定 moves to the second row, as the 0.7.2 note on the landing page says.
