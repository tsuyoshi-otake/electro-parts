# 0.7.5 release: stock counts from M5Stack and Switch Science product pages (#74, PR #75)

The owner asked on 2026-11-04 for the counts that M5Stack official and Switch Science show on their product pages to be recorded (「だめだよ、記録してよ」, then 「その方針で進めて」). 0.7.5 does three things:
- the crawl reads product pages after each complete catalogue walk: every M5Stack product each observation day, and the in-stock Switch Science products one quarter at a time (ADR-0027);
- the published series carries `cappedAt: 100` for Switch Science, and the panel shows a count at the cap as 「100+」;
- the panel says 「在庫数」 instead of 「表示在庫数」, and the chart heading reads 「在庫数の推移（店舗のページが示す数。実在庫を保証しません）」.

This record is the pre-submission check that CLAUDE.md requires. The evidence is kept under `~/tmp/electro-075/`:
- `pages-before/`: the six store manifests before the release;
- `live.mjs`: the 0.7.4 functional script plus C19 (wording), C20 (SS/M5Stack counts) and C21 (「100+」);
- `local-ext/` with `live-local/`: the local 0.7.5 build against the data published before the release;
- `pages-after/`, `main-ext.zip` (built from the merge commit), `pub-ext.zip` and `pub-ext/` (the ZIP Pages serves), `live-pub/`;
- `compat-0.7.4/` and `compat-0.7.3/`: the earlier published ZIPs (`~/tmp/electro-074/pub-ext`, `~/tmp/electro-073/pub-ext`) against the contract 1.2.0 data.
