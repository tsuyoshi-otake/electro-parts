### Dark mode contrast on the Sengoku pages

`evidence/contrast.mjs` counts visible elements whose own text has less than 3:1 contrast with the first opaque background behind it, in dark. It covers the four kinds of page the extension runs on: product, top, category and listing.

- **Before the fix** (`contrast-before.json`): 16 / 64 / 1,278 / 16 elements. The causes:
  - the dark rule painted the blue bar under the logo (`.header_bar`) white, under its yellow greeting
  - the pale boxes stayed light under light text: `#l_category_ttl`, `li.r_menu`, the `h1` headings, `.category_box`, `.goods_item`
  - the red and teal headings of the top page lost their white text
- **The fix** in `userscript/adapters/sengoku.ts`:
  - only `#header` gets white
  - `.header_bar` and the two colored headings keep the shop's colors
  - the pale boxes join the dark surfaces
  - `lightstyles.json` holds the shop's own light colors used for this decision
- **After** (`contrast-after.json`): 0 / 2 / 0 / 0. The two remaining are the teal 「再入荷商品」 heading and its link: white on rgb(8, 190, 190), 2.3:1. The shop draws them that way in light mode too.
- `extension-sengoku.spec.ts` now asserts that dark keeps `.header_bar` at the shop's blue and darkens `h1.item_detail_ttl`. The page-adapter unit test pins the same rules.
  - Fail-before: with the old `:is(#header, .header_bar)` white rule, the E2E failed with "Expected rgb(5, 125, 255), Received rgb(255, 255, 255)" and the unit test failed. Both pass with the fix.
