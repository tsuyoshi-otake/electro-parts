# 0.7.9 release: the extension reads one product's relations from packaged files (#93 option D, PR #109)

0.7.8 carried the whole cross-store relation table (5,631 relations) inside the content script, so every store page parsed 3.8 MB of script, also for a product with no relation. 0.7.9 keeps the table in packaged files and the service worker answers for one product. The design and the measurements are in [ADR-0035](../adr/0035-relations-read-per-product.md). What the panel shows does not change.

This record is the pre-submission check that CLAUDE.md requires. The evidence is kept under `~/tmp/electro-079/`: `live.mjs` (the 0.7.8 script plus C25 and C26), `zip/pub.zip` and `pub-ext/` (the ZIP Pages serves), `live-pub/` (screenshots and `results.json`), `live-run.txt`.

## What this release does not change

- The relation data, the data contract (v1.1), the publisher, the collectors and the workflows. The userscript keeps the table in its bundle and indexes it on the first question; it is not lighter.
- What the panel shows: the same cards, in the same order.

## Not verified

- The worker's own time and memory: the bench reads the page's renderer only.
- Pages that 0.7.8 was not checked on. C25 compares against the 0.7.8 run, so it covers these eight pages.
