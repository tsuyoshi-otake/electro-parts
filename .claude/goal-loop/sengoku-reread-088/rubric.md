# Goal: A Sengoku listing whose total changes while it is being read is read again from its first page, bounded, instead of ending the whole crawl (Issue #88)
Workdir: C:\Codes\tsuyoshi-otake\electro-parts (branch fix/sengoku-listing-reread-088, one commit on top of main)

Context for the verifier: on 2026-11-09 the Sengoku collector aborted the whole crawl because one listing's total changed from 1951 to 1950 between its pages, and that day's sample was lost. The change (`src/collectors/sengoku/collector.ts`) splits the paged read into `readPass` (returns the rows of one pass, keeps nothing) and `readListing` (decides whether to read again). A `ListingChangedError` (only the total changing) discards the pass, waits `SENGOKU_REREAD_SETTLE_MS` (60 s, doubled per further pass, plus jitter of up to half of it) and reads the listing from its first page, up to `maxListingPasses` passes (config, default 2, range 1 to 4). Every other refusal (counter, row count, repeated product) still ends the crawl at once. Rows are recorded only after a pass is complete. Reasons and limits are in the addendum at the end of `docs/adr/0024-sengoku-electronic-parts-category-listings.md`.

Rules for you: do not edit anything in the Workdir. Mutations go in a throwaway copy under `~/tmp/verify-088/` (copy `src`, `tests`, `config`, `package.json`, `tsconfig*.json`, `vitest.config.*` and link `node_modules` with `cmd //c mklink /J`; no git worktrees).
