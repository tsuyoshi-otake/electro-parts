# Goal: Period buttons on the price/stock chart and an estimated sales count for the chosen period (Issue #85)
Workdir: C:\Codes\tsuyoshi-otake\electro-parts (branch feat/chart-period-sales, uncommitted working tree against main)

Context for the verifier: the panel's price chart and stock chart always drew the whole record. The change adds seven period buttons above the chart (1ヶ月 30 days, 3ヶ月 90, 半年 180, 1年 365, 2年 730, 3年 1095, 全期間) that set ONE window shared by the price chart, the stock chart and a new "推定販売数" block under the stock chart. The estimate is the sum of the decreases between consecutive recorded stock counts inside the window (increases are shown apart, never counted as sales). The data contract, publisher, collectors and workflows must not change. Design: docs/adr/0031-chart-periods-and-estimated-sales.md.
