# Goal: the related groups (類似商品, 同一商品候補, 要確認の候補) show their first 3 cards in the same wrapping grid as the store prices and fold the rest (Issue #95)
Workdir: C:\Codes\tsuyoshi-otake\electro-parts (branch feat/related-cards-grid-095, uncommitted or one commit on top of main)

Context for the verifier: before this change `renderRelatedGroups` in `userscript/ui/related.ts` stacked every card of a group vertically (the Raspberry Pi Pico page shows 7 cards / 21 rows). The store price comparison (`renderStorePrices`) already shows its first `VISIBLE_STORE_PRICES` = 3 rows in a `.store-prices` grid (`repeat(auto-fit, minmax(min(200px, 100%), 1fr))`) and folds the rest. The change gives each related group a `.related-cards` grid under the same column rule, shows the first `VISIBLE_RELATED_CARDS` = 3 cards (closest first, the existing order) and folds the rest under `他の<group>(N種類 or N件)` in a `<details data-state-key="related-more-<key>">`. Folded cards stay in the document. Nothing else changes: relations, their order, `MAX_RELATED_HISTORY_LOADS`, the data contract, the extension.
